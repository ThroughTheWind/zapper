# Zapper 0.1.0

The first release of Zapper: a free, open-source remote for Samsung smart TVs, on your Android
phone. It finds your TV on the Wi-Fi, pairs with it once, and gives you a clean remote with a
D-pad you can tap, hold or swipe. Nothing leaves your home network: no account, no ads, no
analytics.

This release is the end of Phase 1 of the [roadmap](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md),
the basic Samsung remote.

## What you can do

- **Find your TV** on the Wi-Fi automatically, or add it by IP address.
- **Pair once.** Accept the prompt on the TV; Zapper explains what to do if the TV refuses or
  times out, and pairs again by itself if the TV forgets the phone.
- **Control it:** power, source, menu, the D-pad, volume and channels, media keys and a number
  pad. Keys your TV does not support are greyed out.
- **Use the phone's volume keys** to change the TV's volume.
- **Keep several TVs**, switch between them, rename them and choose the one Zapper opens on.

## Install

1. On your phone, download `zapper-0.1.0.apk` from the assets below.
2. Open it, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
3. Your phone and your TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Needs Android 8.0 or later and a Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or later). The "Development build"
pre-release is a separate app and can stay installed next to this one.

## Known limits

- Samsung TVs only. Other brands come after 1.0.
- Not yet here: typing into the TV, app shortcuts and turning the TV on (Phase 2), and
  customising the remote's layout, theme and haptics (Phase 3).
- English only.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/issues) with your TV's
model.
