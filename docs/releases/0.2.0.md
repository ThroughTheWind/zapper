# Zapper 0.2.0

Make the remote yours. Zapper 0.2.0 adds the Samsung extras and full customisation: type into
the TV, open your apps in one tap, turn the TV on, and rearrange every key, page and colour.
Still free, open source and local only: no account, no ads, no analytics.

This release covers Phases 2 and 3 of the [roadmap](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md).
More about Zapper on [its website](https://throughthewind.github.io/zapper/).

## What's new

- **Turn the TV on** with the power key, over Wake-on-LAN. If your TV does not wake, Zapper
  explains the one TV setting to enable.
- **App shortcuts:** Netflix and YouTube on the main page, and an Apps page with Prime Video,
  Disney+, Apple TV, Spotify and Plex.
- **Type into the TV** from your phone's keyboard. It opens by itself when the TV shows its own.
- **Source and HDMI 1 to 4 keys** you can add in edit mode, to jump straight to an input.
- **Edit the remote:** tap the pencil to move, resize, add and remove keys, change their label,
  icon and colour, and undo as you go.
- **Pages:** add, rename, reorder and delete them.
- **One layout per TV**, which you can reset, copy from another TV, or export and import as a file.
- **Settings:** theme (including OLED black), colour, key shape, haptics, click sound and repeat
  speed.

## Install or update

Download `zapper-0.2.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. It updates 0.1.0 in place and keeps
your saved TVs. Needs Android 8.0 or later and a Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or later).

## Known limits

- Samsung TVs only. Other brands come after 1.0.
- Turning the TV on over Wi-Fi does not work on every model; Ethernet is more reliable.
- English only.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/issues) with your TV's
model.
