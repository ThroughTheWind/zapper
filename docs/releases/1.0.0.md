# Zapper 1.0.0

Zapper 1.0 is here. On top of everything in 0.2.0, the remote now works with TalkBack and large
text, tells you plainly when the TV cannot be reached, and comes with a guide for adding your own
TV brand. Still free, open source and local only: no account, no ads, no analytics.

This release completes Phase 4, the last phase of the [roadmap](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md).
More about Zapper on [its website](https://throughthewind.github.io/zapper/).

## What's new

- **TalkBack:** every key is read once by what it does, a double tap presses it, and keys say
  they are disabled while no TV is connected. Connection changes are announced.
- **Large text:** key labels follow your phone's font size and shrink to fit instead of being cut
  off, and the welcome screen scrolls.
- **Clearer help when the TV is unreachable:** a banner offers Retry and Switch TV, and tapping a
  key says "Not connected" with Reconnect.
- **Add a TV brand:** a [step-by-step guide](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/blob/main/docs/adapters.md)
  takes contributors from an empty module to a pull request, starting from an example adapter
  that is tested on every build.

## Install or update

Download `zapper-1.0.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. It updates 0.2.0 in place and keeps
your saved TVs and layouts. Needs Android 8.0 or later and a Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or
later).

## Known limits

- Samsung TVs only. Android TV / Google TV, LG and Roku are the next adapters.
- Turning the TV on over Wi-Fi does not work on every model; Ethernet is more reliable.
- English only.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/issues) with your TV's
model.
