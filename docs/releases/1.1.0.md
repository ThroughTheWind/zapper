# Zapper 1.1.0

A small update to 1.0 with two fixes people ran into on real TVs: a working HDMI key for Samsung
TVs that ignore the direct HDMI 1 to 4 keys, and a calmer status while the TV is off. Still free,
open source and local only: no account, no ads, no analytics.

More about Zapper on [its website](https://throughthewind.github.io/zapper/).

## What's new

- **Next HDMI key:** add it from edit mode (System group). Each press moves the TV to its next
  HDMI input. It replaces the HDMI 1 to 4 keys, which Samsung TVs from 2018 on ignore: those keys
  are no longer offered on Samsung, and any you placed stay on your remote, greyed out with the
  reason, so you can swap them for Next HDMI.
- **Calmer status while the TV is off:** the status chip no longer flickers between "TV off" and
  "Connecting" while Zapper checks for the TV in the background, and it connects as soon as the
  TV answers.

## Install or update

Download `zapper-1.1.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. It updates 1.0.0 in place and keeps
your saved TVs and layouts. Needs Android 8.0 or later and a Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or
later).

## Known limits

- Samsung TVs only. Android TV / Google TV, LG and Roku are the next adapters.
- Samsung offers no local way to jump straight to one HDMI port; Next HDMI steps through them.
- Turning the TV on over Wi-Fi does not work on every model; Ethernet is more reliable.
- English only.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/issues) with your TV's
model.
