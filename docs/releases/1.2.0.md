# Zapper 1.2.0

Zapper now works with Android TV and Google TV, its first TV brand after Samsung, and speaks
French, Spanish, German and Polish. Still free, open source and local only: no account, no ads,
no analytics.

More about Zapper on [its website](https://throughthewind.github.io/zapper/).

## What's new

- **Android TV and Google TV:** Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia
  Shield and others. Zapper finds the TV on the Wi-Fi (or add it by IP address), you type the
  6-character code the TV shows once, and then you get the remote with long press, typing into
  the TV and app shortcuts. Thanks to everyone who voted for it and tried the preview.
- **Five languages:** Zapper follows your phone's language in English, French, Spanish, German or
  Polish. On Android 13 and later, Settings > Language lets you pick one for Zapper alone.
- **Digit keys from the catalog** show their digit instead of "DIGIT 1".

## Install or update

Download `zapper-1.2.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. It updates 1.1.0 in place and keeps
your saved TVs and layouts. Needs Android 8.0 or later, and a Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or
later) or an Android TV / Google TV.

## Known limits

- Turning the TV on over the network needs the TV's Wake on LAN or networked standby setting, and
  does not work on every model; Ethernet is more reliable.
- Android TV: the remote does not show the TV's volume or current app yet.
- Samsung offers no local way to jump straight to one HDMI port; Next HDMI steps through them.
- LG and Roku are not supported yet.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind/zapper/issues) with your TV's
model.
