Features

The remote you use every day, on your phone.

Everything here works in the current release.

Finds your TV

Zapper looks for Samsung TVs on your Wi-Fi, even on routers that hide them. If it still can't see yours, add it by its address.

Pair once, then forget about it

Accept Zapper once on the TV. After that it reconnects by itself when you open the app, when Wi-Fi comes back or when the TV wakes up.

Volume keys work

While Zapper is open, your phone's own volume buttons turn the TV up and down, so you don't have to look.

Made for your thumb

Big keys, a round D-pad you can tap or swipe, hold-to-repeat and a light buzz on every press, so your eyes stay on the screen.

All the everyday keys

Power, source, menu, volume, channels, play and pause, and a number pad on its own page. Keys your TV can't take are greyed out.

More than one TV

Save every TV in the house, give them names, switch between them, and pick the one Zapper opens on.

Private by design

Zapper talks to your TV over your home network and nothing else. No account, no cloud, no analytics. Pairing keys are encrypted on your phone, and Zapper checks it is talking to the TV you paired. The code is public, so you can check.

Roadmap

Basics first. The rest is on the way.

Zapper is built in phases: a remote you can rely on every day first, then the extras, then making it yours. Here is where it is.

  1. Phase 1Done

    Basic Samsung remote

    Find, pair and control a Samsung TV every day. In release 0.1.0, tested on a 2019 Samsung RU7020.

    • Find the TV or add it by address
    • Guided, encrypted pairing
    • Automatic reconnection and saved TVs
    • The default remote and number pad
    • Phone volume keys
  2. Phase 2Next

    Samsung extras

    The things a plastic remote can't do well.

    • Type on the TV with your phone keyboard
    • App shortcuts that open TV apps in one tap
    • Turn the TV on, and see whether it is on
  3. Phase 3Planned

    Make it yours

    The remote becomes yours to arrange.

    • Light, dark and true-black themes, colours and haptics
    • Move, resize, add and remove buttons
    • Pages, a layout per TV, undo and reset
    • Export and import layouts
  4. Phase 4Planned

    Release 1.0

    Polish, and an open door for other brands.

    • Accessibility review
    • Onboarding and clearer error help
    • A guide to adding a TV brand

After 1.0

More TV brands, one adapter at a time: Android TV and Google TV first, then LG and Roku. Also on the list: a touchpad mode, a home screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.

Open source

Bring your own TV.

Zapper is GPL-3.0 and built to grow one TV brand at a time. If you can talk to your TV from code, you can add it to Zapper.

  1. 1

    Fork and run it

    Debug builds come with a simulated TV at 192.0.2.1, so you can try the app without any hardware.

  2. 2

    Write an adapter

    Implement TvAdapter in its own adapters/ module. The Samsung adapter is the reference, and the contributing guide lists the steps.

  3. 3

    Open a pull request

    Tell us which TV you tested on. Once merged, it ships in the next release.
